The Science

Why Ozone Treatment Works Where Others Fail

Sprays and air fresheners mask odors temporarily. Ozone (O₃) destroys the odor-causing molecules themselves — permanently neutralizing what you smell at its molecular source.

  • Destroys odor at the molecular sourceO₃ reacts directly with volatile organic compounds, breaking the molecular bonds that create odor — not just covering them up.
  • Reaches where cleaning can'tOzone gas penetrates wall cavities, HVAC ductwork, furniture, subfloors, and every hidden surface where odors reside.
  • Effective on every odor typeSmoke, pet urine, mold, marijuana, cooking, chemical — ozone neutralizes them all through the same oxidation process.
  • No chemicals, no residueOzone reverts to pure oxygen after treatment. No chemical residues, no fragrance masking — just genuinely clean air.

Common Questions About Ozone Odor Removal

Ozone (O₃) is an unstable molecule that readily oxidizes — meaning it reacts with and breaks down other organic compounds. When introduced to a space, ozone gas seeks out volatile organic molecules, chemically alters them, and neutralizes them permanently. The ozone then reverts to oxygen (O₂) leaving no residue.

Most residential treatments take 3–6 hours depending on the size of the space and the severity of the odor. Vehicles typically take 2–3 hours. After the treatment, we ventilate the space for 1–2 hours before it's safe to re-enter.
